The Challenge

Nordwind's old website was slow, difficult to navigate on mobile, and failed to reflect the adventurous spirit of the brand — resulting in high cart abandonment.

UX Redesign

Simplified navigation and checkout flow to reduce friction and drop-off.

Performance

Rebuilt on a modern stack for sub-3-second load times across devices.

Visual Storytelling

Full-bleed imagery and video to capture the outdoor lifestyle brand feel.

The Results

The new site launched with dramatically improved load times and a substantial jump in conversion rate, along with a healthy increase in average order value.
